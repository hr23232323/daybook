# Code of conduct

Daybook should be a calm, useful place to work on unusually sensitive software.
Treat other contributors with care, assume good intent, and critique the work rather
than the person.

We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, intimidation, sexualized attention,
deliberate disruption, or the publication of another person's private information.
That last point matters especially here: never share someone else's financial records,
credentials, tokens, or identifying transaction data.

These expectations apply in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where
someone is representing the project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct puts people or the
project at risk.

Report conduct concerns privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/hr23232323/daybook/security/advisories/new).
Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent practical.
